About China-Lutong

30+ years of diesel fuel injection manufacturing — from a workshop to a global factory trusted in 60+ countries

Home/About

China's Direct-Source Diesel Injection Factory

Putian China-Lutong Machinery Works Co., Ltd. traces its roots to 1982 and was formally established in 1992 in Putian, Fujian Province on China's southeast coast. For over three decades, we have focused exclusively on diesel fuel injection systems — no sideline products, no distractions.

Today, we operate a 3,000–5,000 m² production facility with 80+ precision machines and a workforce of 100–150 skilled technicians and engineers. Our 6,000+ product models cover the complete spectrum — from traditional VE pump head rotors and mechanical nozzles to modern common rail injectors and control valves for Euro 3+ compliant engines.

As an ADS (Association of Diesel Specialists) member since 2002, we are also active members of CAAM, CICEIA, and the National Fuel Injection System Standardization Technical Committee (SAC/TC 396) — contributing to the standards that define our industry.

60–70% of our annual turnover (USD 12–15 million) comes from exports. Our owned brands — DIESEL PARTS, DICSCL PARCS, DIZZO, DIESEL INJECTION PARTS, INJECTRONICS — are trusted by workshops and distributors across six continents.

How We Control Quality

Four inspection gates ensure every part meets OEM specifications before shipping

1. IQC — Incoming

Raw material certification, hardness testing, and dimensional verification before machining begins

2. IPQC — In-Process

Real-time dimensional checks at each machining station — sampling every 50 parts to catch deviations early

3. FQC — Final Inspection

100% functional testing: spray pattern analysis, seal integrity, surface finish, and dimensional conformance

4. OQC — Outgoing Audit

Lot sampling per AQL standards, packaging inspection, and Certificate of Conformance with every shipment
