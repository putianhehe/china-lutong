4-Stage Quality Control

Every component passes four inspection gates before it leaves our factory

IQC — Incoming

Raw material certification & hardness testing before machining begins

IPQC — In-Process

Real-time dimensional checks at each machining station, every 50 parts

FQC — Final

100% functional testing: spray pattern, seal integrity, surface finish

OQC — Outgoing

Lot sampling, packaging inspection & documentation before shipment
